# Card 02 — Which device leads Vago: the Mac or the iPhone?

English copy of `card.ru.md`.

**Question.** Where is each new Vago feature designed and tested first? The other device catches up later. Cards 05 and 08 depend on this.

**Locked.** Decision 01 (your list, pause or block, the toggle, the notice) is already built on the Mac. Security fixes ship to both devices the same day.

**Measure.** Counted minutes: Mac 817 work and 146 waste over 8 days; iPhone 0 every day. Phone waste is unknown.

**Options.**

1. **The Mac leads** (recommended). The guard, the toggle and the notice are designed for the desk. The iPhone shows the day and keeps its current shield.
   *You pay:* phone scrolling stays uncounted for now; a green Mac day can hide an evening on the phone. *You get:* the loop you chose works tomorrow in Chrome, 70% of your time; only the Mac tells work from waste inside a site.
2. **The iPhone leads.** The guard is rebuilt around Apple's Screen Time shield, which the system enforces. The Mac only counts.
   *You pay:* no split inside an app, minutes only in steps; the iPhone has never counted a minute; decision 01 gets rebuilt. *You get:* the strongest lever, on the device that is always with you.

**Test (1 min).** iPhone → Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity → Week. If social and entertainment exceed 30 minutes a day, option 2 fits.
