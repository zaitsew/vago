# Card 01 — What does Vago do so that you procrastinate less?

English copy of `card.ru.md`, the card the owner reads.

**Question.** Vago's core loop on an ordinary workday: start work, drift to a feed, come back, end the day. The other cards depend on it.

**Locked.** No "are you working?" question during the day (your words, 29 Oct). Platform is not picked: the card is drawn for the Mac, the proposed default until card 02.

**Measure and today's number.** Waste minutes per Mac day: median 18 over 6 days (limit 30). Six days, zero actions: 0 focus sessions, 0 corrections, 0 rules. 43% of today's time was unrecognised. The iPhone recorded no minute.

**Options.**

1. **Guard my list** (recommended). Once, in 2 minutes: you confirm up to three places where you lose time (Vago proposes them from your week) and a 30-minute budget. Then a 5-second pause on those places: big button "Close", small "Open for 5 min". Vago decides everything else silently. The evening review stops being part of the loop.
   *You pay:* 2 minutes of setup and a pause a few times a day. *You get:* the mechanism with the best data (one sec: −57% opens; self-set limits: −22 min a day).
2. **Mirror, as now.** Silence by day, a percentage in the menu bar, an evening review.
   *You pay:* nothing. *You get:* silence; in 6 days it changed nothing.
3. **Block in work hours.** The list does not open on a schedule.
   *You pay:* the block hits work (70% of your time is in Chrome). *You get:* a strong effect until you switch it off.

**Test (1 min).** Without opening Vago, name in 30 seconds three places where you lose time. If you can, option 1 fits.
